FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
We understand that adopting AI can feel like a big step — that’s why we’ve answered the most common questions.
How accurate is the AI?
Our AI model is tested over multiple public datasets with 5 Million+ total photos. The average accuracy was 99.9% . In real world testing across events like weddings, marathons etc, our AI model efficiently found guests’ photos from solo photos, group photos, side angle photos and even blurry photos. On a few occasions, we had to zoom into some photos to find where exactly the guest was present, and almost every time, AI managed to find the person in a cluttered photo, where the naked eye failed.
But don’t take our word for it. We encourage you to take the Free Trial and try the magic of AI yourself! 🙂
How long does it take for guests to get their photos after clicking a selfie?
Guests get their photos almost instantaneously after clicking the selfie. Our blazing fast AI makes photo viewing experience a breeze for all your guests.
What’s the maximum size of photo that I can upload?
The maximum size of a photo that you can upload is 30 MB.
Are my photos compressed after uploading?
No, we store every photo in its original resolution. However, you can still compress the photos to your desired resolution before uploading to TurtlePic and we will retain the original resolution that you upload, regardless of its size.
What is the difference between Agency and Individual account?
Agency account plans are based on storage and available for monthly / yearly subscriptions. These are designed to be suited for businesses like event photographers, corporates, universities etc. that host / cover multiple events throughout the year.
Individual account plans are based on storage and available for yearly plans based on the number of photos. These plans are designed for individuals that host an event within their home(s) and do not have multiple events throughout the year.
Are my photos and other data secure?
At TurtlePic, security is one of the top priorities. Your photos and data are safely stored in enterprise grade encryption standards at rest, and travels in encrypted format over the internet. With multiple user managed privacy settings on top, you can be assured that every bit of your data is protected. To know more, read our privacy policy
here
.
In Agency Account, can I delete older events to upload new events in place of it?
Yes, the storage in Agency Accounts is fully reusable and you can delete and upload new data in your account any number of times.
Are there any limits on downloads or number of guests that can access their photos?
No, you get to enjoy truly unlimited photo sharing experience with TurtlePic in all our paid plans. To know more, refer our pricing page
here
.
