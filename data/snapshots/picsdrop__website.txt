FAQ
Find answers to your questions and learn more about our free image hosting.
What type of images can be uploaded to PicsDrop?
Any image file can be uploaded to PicsDrop.
Uploaded files must be legal and must not contain any copyrighted material or prohibited content.
What is the time limit for image uploads in PicsDrop?
The time limit for guest uploads is two days. This means the image will automatically be deleted after two days.
While for registered users, it can be unlimited time.
How does image password protection work?
It means when you share your image, the viewer must input the correct password to view the image.
You can change or remove the password on your account settings.
Is my account secured?
We do our best to make your account secure.
For an extra layer of protection, you can enable 2-factor authentication (2FA) in your account settings.
Image file size that can be uploaded.
Guest upload can only upload up to
5MB
for each image, while registered users can upload up to
100MB
for each image.
