What is ShootProof?
ShootProof is a comprehensive platform for photographers to share, sell, and manage their work. It combines
client galleries
with business tools like
booking
,
contracts and invoicing
to streamline your workflow from booking to final delivery.
Does ShootProof take a commission on my sales?
No. ShootProof offers
0% commission
on all sales. You keep 100% of your profits (minus standard credit card processing fees) and maintain full control over your own markup and pricing.
How does the photo storage pricing work?
Pricing is based on your
total photo count
rather than file size or GBs. You can choose a plan that fits your current portfolio—from 100 photos to
Unlimited
—and upgrade or downgrade as your library changes.
How does print fulfillment work?
ShootProof integrates with top professional labs like
Bay Photo, WHCC, and Miller’s,
along with international labs. When a client orders a print from your gallery, the order is automatically sent to the lab, printed, and shipped directly to their door.
Can I customize the galleries with my brand?
Yes. You can use your own
logo, brand colors, and custom subdomains
. You can also apply
automatic watermarks
to your preview images, which are removed only after a client completes a purchase.
What are the pricing options for ShootProof?
ShootProof offers a free plan (up to 100 photos) and several paid tiers (billed annually):
1,500 photos:
$8.33/mo
5,000 photos:
$16.67/mo
25,000 photos:
$26.67/mo

$50.00/mo
Note: Monthly billing options are also available at a slightly higher rate.
