## Company Overview

Google Photos is a cloud-based photo and video storage service that allows users to edit, organize, search, and share their memories. It offers 15 GB of free storage for Google Account users and integrates with devices from both Android and iOS platforms. The target audience appears to be consumers who want to manage and preserve their personal photos and videos.

## Strengths

* Google Photos has a robust suite of AI-powered tools that can enhance and edit photos, giving customers more creative control.

## Weaknesses

* Google Photos lacks specific features designed for professional photographers, leaving an opportunity for ShutterCloud to focus on these needs.
* The service has limited capabilities for client management and delivery, making it less suitable for photographers who rely heavily on these aspects of their business.
