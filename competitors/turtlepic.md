## Company Overview

TurtlePic is an AI-powered photo sharing platform for events, aiming to make event photo sharing seamless and effortless. Its target audience includes event hosts, photographers, and businesses that organize events such as weddings, corporate events, sports, music festivals, schools, colleges, conferences, exhibitions, and trade shows. TurtlePic positions itself as a trusted provider of AI-powered galleries with advanced features like face recognition, privacy controls, and seamless photo sharing.
