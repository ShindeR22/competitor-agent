## Company Overview

FotoOwl is an AI-powered platform specializing in online client photo galleries and media asset management. It targets photographers and event organizers across diverse sectors such as weddings, sports, music festivals, corporate events, and schools. The platform aims to streamline workflows, enhance client engagement through AI face recognition and personalized reels, and enable photo monetization, positioning itself as an all-in-one solution for capturing, organizing, and distributing media.
