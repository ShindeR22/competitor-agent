## Company Overview

PhotoMall is an AI-powered photo sharing and delivery platform targeting professional photographers and event organizers. It specializes in automatically sorting and delivering event photos using AI face recognition and QR codes, aiming for instant, accurate, and secure delivery. PhotoMall positions itself as a comprehensive solution for photographers, offering tools for client management, a custom-branded mobile app, event live streaming, a photography website, and billing software.
