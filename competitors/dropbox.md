## Company Overview

Dropbox is a cloud storage and file sharing service that enables users to store, share, and access files across devices. Its target audience includes individuals and businesses looking for secure and collaborative solutions for storing and managing their digital content. Dropbox positions itself as a reliable and user-friendly platform for both personal and professional use.
