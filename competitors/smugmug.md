## Pricing Changes

**Baseline Pricing:**

SmugMug offers four plans:

1. **Direct**: $20/MO (billed annually) - unlimited storage, digital delivery, streamlined upload, and 24/7 human support.
2. **Portfolio**: $23.50/MO (billed annually) - includes Direct plan features, plus print and digital download sales, customizable website templates, watermark and photo theft protection tools, and full-service customer order fulfillment.
3. **Pro**: $37.00/MO (billed annually) - includes Portfolio plan features, plus multiple custom pricelists for photos and galleries, client favoriting and download limits, and expert support.
4. **Enterprise** (new): a tailor-made plan for serious volume and specialized needs.

There are no changes since the last snapshot as this is the first run of the report. 

The pricing positions itself as premium, given the extensive features and high price points compared to other photo delivery platforms.
